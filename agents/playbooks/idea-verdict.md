# Playbook: assess an opportunity

Adapted for GlobalLand from `saas-idea-validator` in [cbrock84/headcount](https://github.com/cbrock84/headcount) (MIT, © 2026 Chris Brock). See THIRD_PARTY.md.

## When to use
Optional checklist for assessing an opportunity, not a gate before every mission or experiment. It requires no product or platform. A bounded experiment can itself supply missing evidence.

## Questions to consider
- **Problem:** who has it, how often, and what workaround do they use or pay for?
- **Market:** where do customers seek help or spend money? Check sources suited to the strategy; no platform is preferred.
- **Competition:** check alternatives and evidence of customer budgets.
- **Monetization:** who pays for what result, on what terms? Choose suitable pricing and compare receipts with total costs.
- **Defensibility and execution:** can agents keep it working when the target site or API changes?

## Give one verdict
When useful: Pursue (and a test), Pursue if (a condition to check), Reframe, or Do not pursue (a reason). State uncertainty and the next evidence needed. Record findings with WORLD record_lesson to avoid repeating investigations.

## Red flags
Problem stated as the solution; nobody pays anything today; "AI-powered" is the only advantage; buyer is "everyone"; revenue depends on one platform's goodwill with no fallback.
