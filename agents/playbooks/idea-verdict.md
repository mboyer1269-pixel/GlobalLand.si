# Playbook: assess an opportunity

Adapted for GlobalLand from `saas-idea-validator` in [cbrock84/headcount](https://github.com/cbrock84/headcount) (MIT, © 2026 Chris Brock). See THIRD_PARTY.md.

## When to use
Optional checklist for opportunities you choose. A bounded experiment can supply missing evidence.

## Questions to consider
- **Demand:** who pays, for what outcome, and what alternative do they use today? Cite the evidence.
- **Delivery:** what is the smallest acceptable result? Check acceptance terms, prerequisites and authorized submission access before substantial work.
- **Settlement:** how can GlobalLand actually receive payment? Check eligibility, payment route, fees and earliest plausible settlement. An advertised amount does not establish funding or receipt.
- **First revenue:** compare feasible options by time to settlement, evidence of demand and total cost. If a step is blocked, name it precisely and keep looking for a feasible path.

## Give one verdict
When useful: Pursue (and a test), Pursue if (a condition to check), Reframe, or Do not pursue (a reason). Record the next useful action and durable findings with WORLD record_lesson.
