# Playbook: idea verdict before building

Adapted for GlobalLand from `saas-idea-validator` in [cbrock84/headcount](https://github.com/cbrock84/headcount) (MIT, © 2026 Chris Brock). See THIRD_PARTY.md.

## When to use
Before creating a mission that builds a product, an Actor or a page. Building is cheap for agents; distribution and willingness to pay are not.

## Interrogate in this order
- **Problem:** who has it, how often, what do they do today instead? The strongest signal is a paid workaround (a spreadsheet, a contractor, an existing paid tool).
- **Market:** where do these buyers already search? For an Apify Actor: is there demand visible in the Store (similar Actors with users), in forums or in job posts?
- **Competition:** established competitors prove budget exists. "Nobody does this" usually means nobody pays.
- **Monetization:** who pays, how much per event, and is the price above platform costs?
- **Defensibility and execution:** can agents keep it working when the target site or API changes?

## Give one verdict
Pursue (and the cheapest test), Pursue if (the condition and how to check it), Reframe (the stronger adjacent idea), or Do not pursue (one reason). "It depends" is not a verdict. Record it with WORLD record_lesson.

## Red flags
Problem stated as the solution; nobody pays anything today; "AI-powered" is the only advantage; buyer is "everyone"; revenue depends on one platform's goodwill with no fallback.
