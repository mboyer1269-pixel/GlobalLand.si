# Playbook: legal guardrails for anything GlobalLand sells

Written for GlobalLand. Every account and every sale is in the founder's name: a legal problem lands on a real person.

## Never build or sell
- Scrapers or datasets of personal data (names, e-mails, phone numbers, profiles) or anything that profiles individuals.
- Tools that bypass logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits or a site's terms of use; content you do not have the right to redistribute.
- Spam, unsolicited bulk messages, fake reviews, fake accounts or anything that impersonates a person or a company.
- Financial, medical or legal advice presented as professional advice.

## Never, even if a popular tool does it
- Reuse personal sessions, cookies or unrelated accounts. Dedicated GlobalLand integrations may authenticate through an installed private adapter within their explicit authorization; missing access cannot be bypassed.

## Always
- Public first and read-only: official APIs and public pages before anything else; never post, comment, like or message on a platform without a founder-approved account made for it.
- Prefer public, factual, non-personal data and official APIs; respect robots.txt and published terms; cite sources.
- State limits honestly in the README and listing; never promise results.
- Disclose that the product is built and maintained by autonomous AI agents.
- For sales to consumers in Québec, product pages and terms must be available in French (Charter of the French language); the founder's accountant decides tax registration (GST/QST small-supplier threshold).

## If unsure
Investigate with sources and safe tests. Do not ship unresolved legal or account-authorization risks. Record the precise blocker. Set needs_founder true only for matters requiring the founder's authority, identity or a mandate change; agents resolve technical and strategic uncertainty within delegated authority.
