# Playbook: find the one constraint on revenue

Adapted for GlobalLand from `business-growth-consultant` in [cbrock84/headcount](https://github.com/cbrock84/headcount) (MIT, © 2026 Chris Brock). See THIRD_PARTY.md.

## When to use
When assessing flat revenue or prioritizing further work.

Optional diagnostic; adapt it to your chosen activity. It requires no business model or fixed sequence.

## Check these signals
1. Discovery: can intended customers find and understand the offer?
2. Conversion: do they request, order or use it?
3. Delivery: does it produce the promised result?
4. Retention: do customers return or refer others, when relevant?
5. Economics: do verified receipts cover costs? Quotes are not settled money.

Find the binding constraint from evidence; rank moves by impact, confidence and effort. Independent experiments may run in parallel within resources and dependencies. Define evidence for continuing, changing or abandoning the approach.
