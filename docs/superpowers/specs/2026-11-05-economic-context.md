# Economic context

Follow-on design, 2026-11-05. Read the economic gap note, active roadmap and source; no active implementation files changed. Add one read-only projection shared by director and STATUS. No new ledger, authority, budget, financial action or prerequisite for useful free work.

**Interfaces.** Add `Treasury.economic_snapshot() -> dict` in `scripts/treasury.py` and `economic.project(root, bank) -> dict` in new `scripts/economic.py`. The former returns `{snapshot, journal, commitments}` from one read-only SQLite transaction (`mode=ro`, `BEGIN`, existing `_view(db)`); do not instantiate another Treasury, call its schema migration, verifier or `_finish`. The latter returns `{snapshot, context}`: snapshot is the unchanged authoritative view; context contains `{journal, commitments, receivables, cost_coverage}`. It adds the existing safe receivables derivation from a strictly validated single optional-file read, plus fixed unknown-cost metadata. Director `prepare_context` and `tools.Toolbox.run` STATUS use the returned snapshot for their existing treasury/bank field and the same projection for `economic_context`. Preserve existing failure behavior for unreadable authoritative snapshots; optional journal/receivable failures yield UNAVAILABLE/null, never verified zero. Public bank-file enrichment can wait.

**Verified sources.** `treasury.py:37–53` stores `operations.amount` as integer cents, status text, and `events.body` as JSON text with digest/action/operation_id; there is no amount column on events. `_evidence` (`:139–158`) admits settled CAD receipts and deduplicates `(provider,account,event_id)`. Parse validated bodies; check digest, identity, exact integer amount (exclude bool), currency and kind/action correspondence. Sum in Python integers, avoiding SQLite SUM overflow for lifetime turnover. Never re-verify receipts through a network adapter.

`journal` contains `state`, `history_coverage`, fixed `coverage_reason`, and `known_funding_cents`, `known_revenue_credits_cents`, `known_spend_debits_cents`, `known_expense_refunds_cents`, `known_customer_reversals_cents`. Map FUNDING/FUNDING, REVENUE/CREDIT, SPEND/SPEND, REFUND/CREDIT, REVERSAL/DEBIT. POSITIONS and CANCELLED events contribute no cash flow; cancelled operations have status RELEASED. SPEND includes investments: call it a debit, not necessarily an operating cost. Gross known revenue credits differ from authoritative `snapshot.revenue_cents`, which decreases on REVERSAL with per-event flooring (`:220`). Do not subtract `founder_attested_revenue_cents` to invent automatically verified net revenue.

`commitments` exposes current `held_cents` and `unknown_cents` separately from operations statuses HELD/UNKNOWN; neither is realized expenditure. Keep snapshot available/maturing/reserve_floor/positions unchanged.

**Coverage.** No genesis/completeness marker exists. Missing FUNDING evidence when funded, SETTLED operations lacking matching SPEND, or `funding + revenue + refunds − spend − reversals != snapshot.cash_cents` proves INCOMPLETE. Otherwise coverage remains UNKNOWN, including an empty database: consistency cannot exclude missing offsetting events. Never label COMPLETE or infer spending as `5000 − cash`. Valid known-event totals remain labeled known under INCOMPLETE; malformed/unreadable history makes totals null/UNAVAILABLE. No balance repair or invented opening entry.

**Receivables and unknowns.** Reuse the exact safe fields from `payouts.py:118–122`: integer `apify_receivable_usd_cents`, validated YYYY-MM `apify_receivable_months`, fixed nonspendable note. Keep USD separate; no CAD conversion or addition to cash. Malformed projection becomes UNAVAILABLE/null.

Set `fees_cents`, `profit_cents`, `margin`, `experiment_costs` to null with explicit UNKNOWN/UNALLOCATED labels. Settled totals may already include fees; never add estimated fees again. Existing founder-provided VPS and model subscriptions are outside GlobalLand's 50 CAD: amount, currency, period and allocation remain null/UNKNOWN; no Treasury debit, monthly total or token-based estimate. Raw accounts, receipt bodies/digests, requests, operation IDs and payment references never leave the read layer.

Detailed fee splits, investment cost basis, per-experiment allocation and documented subscription periods may wait until a transaction needs them. This view must not claim profit before those uncertainties are resolved.

## Exact acceptance tests

1. Use admitted fixtures: FUNDING 5000, REVENUE 1000, settled SPEND 250, REFUND 50, REVERSAL 100; then HELD 200 and submitted UNKNOWN 300. With ACTIVE state, zero maturity/floor, assert cash `5000+1000-250+50-100 == 5700`, available `5700-200-300 == 5200`, reserved 500, net revenue 900, known gross revenue 1000, debits/refunds/reversals 250/50/100, commitments 200/300. Coverage UNKNOWN, not COMPLETE. Apify invoice 2340 USD cents changes neither cash nor available.
2. Restart and replay identical receipts: every aggregate unchanged. A settled debit below its original reservation contributes its receipt amount, not reserved maximum. Investment SPEND remains a debit; POSITIONS and cancellation add no cash debit. A reversal larger than prior revenue followed by later revenue must use authoritative snapshot net revenue, not `max(total credits − total reversals,0)`.
3. Missing funding event, missing settled-operation SPEND and a legacy cash/history mismatch each produce INCOMPLETE without balance mutation. Remove equal offsetting credit/debit events that leave cash consistent: coverage must still never become COMPLETE. Corrupt JSON, digest/type/action mismatch or missing events table yields null journal totals/UNAVAILABLE; readable snapshot and commitments remain usable.
4. Add maturity 100 and reserve floor 50 to the cash fixture: available becomes 5050, cash remains 5700. HALTED/EXTINCT remain authoritative and never become spendable through this view. A founder-attested credit followed by reversal never produces an inferred API-only revenue amount.
5. Director and STATUS projections match with frozen fixture state. Receipt/account/request/reference sentinels never occur in either serialized context. Projection invokes no verifier, finance integration, writes, schema migration or state transition; assert tables unchanged. Receivable read failure is UNKNOWN/null, not zero. All subscription period/allocation/profit fields remain unknown and no monthly amount appears.


## Binding constraints and integration

Python3.12+ standard library only; no network, schema/balance migration, verifier invocation, new payment capability or authority change. Reading this view is not a prerequisite for free useful work. Root owns Git; code readiness, deployment and economic outcomes stay separate.

`economic.project(root,bank)` obtains one `bank.economic_snapshot()` and returns its snapshot plus the safe context. For documented lightweight Treasury test doubles with only `snapshot()`, preserve the authoritative snapshot and explicitly mark economic details UNAVAILABLE/null; do not fabricate historical zero. Production Treasury implements the new method. Existing authoritative snapshot failures still propagate. STATUS and director consume the same snapshot/context pair; avoid an extra snapshot call solely to assemble these fields. Existing stop checks are unchanged.

Journal state is AVAILABLE or UNAVAILABLE; history_coverage UNKNOWN or INCOMPLETE when readable, never COMPLETE. Commitments expose state/held_cents/unknown_cents. Receivables expose state plus the existing exact safe fields; missing unreadable or malformed optional data is null with a fixed unavailable reason. `cost_coverage` records null fees_cents/profit_cents/margin/experiment_costs and fixed UNKNOWN/UNALLOCATED labels, with founder-provided existing services marked outside the50CADcapital and amount/currency/period/allocation unknown. No free-form source text or exception enters the view. Economic projections may be inspected via existing tool and director only; public bank-file enrichment is deferred.

Use the exact stored canonical JSON digest convention from Treasury._evidence; compare event identity columns with body identity and validate kind/action/operation correspondence. RELEASED reservations and CANCELLED receipts are not spend debits; SETTLED matching checks must not compare actual spend to reserved maximum as an equality. No-verifier read means these are known admitted event totals, not fresh provider verification.

## Preflight clarifications (binding)

Receivables use one strict read of `state/private/payouts.json`. Missing, unreadable, malformed, duplicate-key or invalid-entry input yields UNAVAILABLE/null; a valid existing empty object yields AVAILABLE/zero. Validate month keys against payouts.MONTH, object entries and RECEIVABLE/PAID status. RECEIVABLE invoice amounts must be exact integers (not bool), positive and at most MAX_PAYOUT_CENTS (10,000,000). Preserve valid PAID entries without an invoice, as force-recorded payouts can create those. Extend `payouts.receivables(root, *, entries=None)` compatibly: None preserves old behavior; a supplied validated dictionary uses existing safe derivation without reloading. No private entries enter context.

Only bank, journal and commitments share the SQLite transaction. The separately recorded invoice ledger may lag bank settlement (bank credit is committed before invoice PAID). Label it `INDEPENDENT_INVOICE_LEDGER_MAY_LAG_SETTLEMENT` with a fixed explanation; never present a recorded receivable as proof money is still unpaid or add it to cash, revenue or profit. No payout-write repair or cross-store transaction in this increment.

Extend existing acceptance scenarios with missing/empty/boolean/negative/duplicate-key invoice data, PAID without invoice, helper no-reload, and stale RECEIVABLE beside already-admitted bank cash. SQLite read-only scope applies to the new projection; unchanged existing stop-guard snapshot reads remain allowed. Actual spend may exceed reservation and HALT the bank while remaining a valid admitted debit; zero-reservation release without a receipt remains valid.
