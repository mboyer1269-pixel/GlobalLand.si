# GlobalLand AI employees — goal milestones

The founder authorized a development team to implement the commercial-autonomy analysis. This roadmap preserves the full goal; completing the first increment is not completion of the whole goal.

## Direction

Agents choose markets, products, platforms and experiments. Improve their ability to act and learn from real economic consequences. Keep the existing financial boundary and truthful evidence. No new purchases, prospect messages, account agreements or identity steps are implied by a development task.

## Milestones

- [x] Private commercial feedback reaches the director safely, with technical activity, interpreted market signals and verified money distinguished. Implemented in PR23; synthetic inputs remain excluded from real market signals.
- [ ] Customer/thread continuity retains relevant commitments and prior work without mixing customers or granting incoming mail new authority.
- [ ] Event-driven initiative resumes useful work durably without duplicate effects or unbounded wakeups; periodic cycles remain a recovery path.
- [ ] Economic context separates cash, costs, fees, refunds, commitments and unknowns; founder subscription costs use explicit periods/allocation rather than invented monthly totals.
- [ ] Agent-selected commercial experiment has a documented usable acquisition and collection path, with missing access surfaced precisely and supported actions verified. A publication alone does not prove audience or revenue.
- [ ] Independent workers are implemented only for a concrete beneficial parallel task, with isolated contexts, clear ownership, bounded resources and integration of results. WORLD registration alone must not be described as a running worker.
- [ ] Full review, appropriate automated checks and controlled end-to-end evidence; production installation and actual commercial outcomes reported separately from code readiness.
