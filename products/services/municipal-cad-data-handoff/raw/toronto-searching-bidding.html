Searching & Bidding on City Contracts

The City does business with suppliers registered on the SAP Business Network. Suppliers search for and bid on City solicitations using the SAP Business Network, which allows them to download solicitations documents for free and upload more complete bids.

New suppliers who have not previously registered with the City, and who are interested in viewing and bidding on City solicitations, can register by going to the City's Supplier Registration Form. Notices of Intended Procurement are advertised on the City’s website through the Toronto Bids Portal (TO Bids). Selecting a posting will take you to the SAP Business Network portal for bidding.
