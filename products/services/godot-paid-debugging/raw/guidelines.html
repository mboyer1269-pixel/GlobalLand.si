Community Rules

All official community spaces (forum, reddit, discord and youtube) are maintained by the Godot Foundation and follow our Godot Code of Conduct.

The rules below are the general baseline, but platform specific rules may vary. For example reddit has rules about flairs, which do not make sense for other platforms. So make sure to check the rules on the other platforms if you plan to use them.

Breaking rules marked with will get you banned immediately.

About moderation

  • Don’t engage when someone breaks the rules, inform the mod team instead. Even if you are unsure whether the rules apply in a certain situation, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance, we are here to help but cannot do that if we are unaware.
  • We may send warnings via the platform tools. We don’t count strikes, and not all warnings are bad! They simply help us inform you of important things privately.
  • Do not question moderation decisions (e.g. being told to drop a topic, receiving a warning) publicly to avoid derailing the conversation. We are happy to give you further explanation if you contact the mod team instead.
  • Do not message individual persons of the mod team, instead always contact the mod team. This means that more people can answer you.

Quickly reporting posts to the mod team

Posts that violate our community rules or are otherwise inappropriate should be reported. You can do so by clicking the small flag icon below the offending post. Do not engage with these posts, it only brings more attention to them.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding forum moderation, please reach out to our @moderators. You can find a “Message” button on the moderators group page in the top right corner.

General Expectations

DO:

  • speak English (for moderation purposes);
  • act like you would in any professional environment;
  • use clear language and avoid confusing slang or humor, as this community is international;
  • keep conversations “workplace appropriate”, don’t discuss elections, mention recent news only when gamedev related, keep in mind there may be minors in this space, etc.;
  • read category descriptions to be sure you are in the proper place for what you intend to post about;
  • assume that people (members and moderators alike) are well-meaning first and foremost, ask for clarification before reacting in an offended manner;
  • give feedback only if requested, or ask if it is wanted (some people only want to share progress).

DON’T:

  • disrespect mod team members or foundation staff ;
  • debate moderators on which conversations are or aren’t appropriate to have;
  • troll or purposely incite fights ;
  • be rude, condescending, mocking;
  • use discriminatory language or make jokes based on someone’s identity (including, but not limited to: race, age, sexuality or gender, disability, …) ;
  • engage with bad faith actors, contact the mod team to handle the situation instead.

Tech Support

Helping is voluntary, you do not need to answer every question you see. If you do, then do not expect any compensation in return.

DO:

  • include all relevant details so that other users can help you (see “How to get help” for tips);
  • avoid immediately answering “how do I do A” with “do B instead”, try letting the asker explain their intentions first.

DON’T:

  • disregard questions, e.g. by saying “just google it” or “ask ChatGPT”;
  • ridicule people for asking questions you deem to be simple or obvious;
  • ask for money in return for help;
  • belittle a user because of their personal choices or preferences (i.e. using a certain tool over another).

Generative AI

This space is for people to discuss with people and help people.

DO:

  • keep AI discourse in the non-help categories and keep it respectful while not breaking other rules;
  • be respectful of users who rely on machine translation tools as not everyone is a proficient English speaker.

DON’T:

  • post AI-generated content (including code, dialogue, music, etc);
  • ask for help or provide help debugging AI-generated code (as it’s a waste of volunteer time and effort);
  • advertise AI services;
  • be a middleman passing other’s questions and problems into generative or agentic AI, and copy-pasting its output back (a.k.a, “don’t be a meatproxy”);
  • be an AI-run account as we will treat you like any other unapproved bot .

Self-Promotion

DO:

  • show off your Godot projects in the showcase category;
  • share resources for Godot Engine learners in the resources category;
  • be supportive of each other’s achievements;
  • reach out to the mod team before advertising anything else (e.g. events) and wait for permission/denial.

DON’T:

  • post job offers;
  • ask people to do free work for you;

Health & Safety

DO:

  • be mindful sharing personal information (age, location, etc) as this is a public space;
  • keep in mind that there might be minors in this space and interact accordingly;
  • when in doubt, use content warnings & spoiler tags;
  • accept differences in communication needs or styles (e.g. related to neurodiversity);
  • prefer positivity and try to raise each other up.

DON’T:

  • disregard or ridicule mental or physical health issues;
  • give, ask for, or take medical advice;
  • post about or encourage illegal behavior;
  • disclose being a minor (including in your profile).
