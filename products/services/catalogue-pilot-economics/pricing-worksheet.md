# Catalogue QA pilot pricing worksheet

CAD 60 is the provisional fee recorded in CATALOGUE-COMMISSION-TERMS-APPROVAL. Use this worksheet to scope an eligible inquiry and check actual terms approval before quoting. The scenarios are planning assumptions. Fees, allocated subscription costs, actual effort, sales and settlement are unknown.

Include qualification, input checks, analysis, report review, one scoped correction if promised, and operator order and collection work in total time. Include prospecting time attributable to the sale when measuring results.

Cash-cost headroom equals proposed fee minus modeled time cost. A negative value misses the selected hourly target even before direct cash costs. For example, 90 minutes at CAD 45/hour leaves a CAD 7.50 shortfall before fees. Subtract actual direct costs before evaluating contribution and assess shared costs separately before drawing any profitability conclusion.

Operator fields for a real eligible inquiry

- Record the requested checks and estimated minutes for each activity above.
- Record applicable direct costs or mark them unknown. Use the chosen hourly target to decide whether to narrow scope or seek different terms before acceptance.
- Confirm buyer access to the administrative CAD Interac route before relying on it. Record authorization, acceptance, delivery and verified settlement separately.
