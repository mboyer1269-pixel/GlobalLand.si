Interac e-Transfer® Business Request Money

The simple, fast and secure way for businesses to request and collect payments

Simplify how you get paid

The Interac e-Transfer service you trust, now streamlined for business payments. Interac e-Transfer Business Request Money helps you quickly and securely collect payments from customers, enabling direct payment from your website, app, QR code, or even directly within your invoices.

Ways to use Business Request Money

Storefront purchases

Small businesses can accept payment for goods and services at their storefront using a QR code-driven Request Money transaction.

Donations

Non-profit organizations can accept payments online or in person using a QR code-driven Business Request Money transaction.

Payments within a merchant’s website or app

Businesses can initiate payment transactions within their platforms - website or mobile app - without requiring customers to log into a separate banking channel.

Funding a new account or digital wallet

Consumers can open a Canadian deposit or investment account (RRSP, TFSA) at a participating financial institution or an account with a licensed lottery and gaming operator and fund it instantly by initiating a Business Request Money transaction.

Credit card payments

Your customers can now pay their credit card bills by sending you a Request Money transaction – directly from their banking app.

Utility payments

Utility companies can accept monthly bill payments from their customers by initiating a Business Request Money transaction through their company platform.

Seamless. Straightforward. Secure.

1

Fast, frictionless experience

Businesses benefit from instant payment deposits. ​And customers enjoy the flexibility of transacting directly through a website or mobile app.

2

Low acceptance cost

Business Request Money can be a more cost-effective alternative compared to other payment methods, such as credit cards, while enabling your business to receive funds instantly.

3

State-of-the-art security

Pre-established payment consent reduces the risk of fraud and directs deposits to a single source. Plus, Business Request Money brings the same bank-grade security as other Interac e-Transfer services.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the Business Request Money feature at my physical storefront or pop-up?

Yes. Consumers can pay for goods and services at a storefront or any small business using a QR code-driven Business Request Money transaction.

What are the requirements to set up Interac e-Transfer Business Request Money?

Business Request Money is available through a participating financial institution’s online banking service. Organizations looking to incorporate the feature on their website, mobile app or via a QR code should contact their financial institution.

Is Interac e-Transfer Business Request Money secure?

Yes. Pre-established payment consent reduces the risk of fraud and directs deposits to a single source. Plus, Business Request Money offers the same bank-grade security as other Interac e-Transfer services. Funds are transferred through established and secure banking procedures.

Which financial institutions currently offer Interac e-Transfer Business Request Money?

Business Request Money is currently available through the following participating Canadian financial institutions:

  • BMO
  • CIBC
  • DC Bank
  • Peoples Trust
  • Scotiabank

More financial institutions are working to bring Business Request Money to their business customers, so this list will continue to grow.

What if my financial institution isn't on the list yet?

You still have options. If you bank with an institution that doesn't yet offer Business Request Money, we recommend two things:

1. Let your financial institution know you're interested. Merchant demand is one of the biggest signals financial institutions use when deciding which features to bring to their business customers.

2. Stay in the loop with us. Sign up above and we'll keep you informed as new financial institutions come on board - so you'll know as soon as it's available where you do your banking.
