Data capture software FAQs

What is data capture software?

Data capture software collects the invoices, receipts and statements a business produces and turns them into structured records — supplier, date, amount, tax and line detail — without anyone re-entering them by hand. Dext does more than read a document. It categorises what it reads, applies the rules your practice has set, and delivers the record to the accounting software you already use at over 99% accuracy.

How do I extract data from invoices and receipts?

Clients photograph them in the Dext app, forward them to a dedicated email address, send them by WhatsApp, or drop them in from a desktop. Dext reads each one, pulls out the supplier, date, totals and line detail, and publishes it where your ledger expects it.

Can AI handle invoice data entry?

Yes. Dext's models were built on over 2 billion accounting documents, so they cope with the ordinary invoice and the awkward one — crumpled, handwritten, several pages long. The intelligent automation does the repetitive work and your expertise decides what gets applied.

What should I look for in data capture software?

Accuracy first, including on handwritten and crumpled documents, because a correction costs a practice more time than the capture ever saved. Then coverage past capture itself — categorisation, duplicate detection and publishing — so a document does not stop halfway. And finally fit, so the software works the way your practice and your clients already work rather than the other way around. Dext extracts at over 99% accuracy, on the tidy documents and the ones that have been in a coat pocket for a month.

How accurate is Dext’s OCR?

Over 99% across the fields that matter: date, amount, supplier, tax, currency and invoice number. The models are retrained continuously on accounting documents rather than general text, which is why the accuracy compounds instead of plateauing.

Is my data secure and compliant?

Yes. Documents are encrypted, held in Vault, our intelligent document storage, and kept audit-ready with the evidence attached to the record it belongs to. Dext is ISO/IEC 27001 certified and GDPR compliant, and all customer data is stored in the EU.

How do I get started with Dext data capture?

Start a 14-day trial, connect your accounting software and put one client's documents through it. There is no card to enter and nothing to unpick if you decide to stop.

Does the data publish into Xero and QuickBooks?

Yes, Through the deep integration with Xero and QuickBooks, Dext is able to publish the data straight into your accounting software.
