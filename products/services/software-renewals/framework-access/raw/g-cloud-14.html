G-Cloud 14

All public sector organisations and charities can use this online catalogue to buy cloud-based computing services, including hosting, software, cloud support, and many off-the-shelf, pay-as-you-go cloud solutions.

Agreement number: RM1557.14

Start date: 29/10/2024

End date: 28/10/2026

Regulation: PCR2015

Agreement type: PCR15 Framework

Updates

29 October 2026: Please be advised that G-Cloud 14 expired on 28 October 2026. You should now access RM1557.15 G-Cloud 15 for your cloud requirements. Existing G-Cloud 14 call-off contracts signed prior to the 28 October 2026 expiration date will remain in effect for their full defined terms. Work performed under G-Cloud 14 cannot be transferred to G-Cloud 15 agreements. For the most current information, please refer to the official G-Cloud 15 webpage.

Documents

Each section below has the links to the documents, what they are for, when to use them and if you need to enter any information into them.

You might not need all the contractual documents to buy the products or services from this agreement.

Updates

Crown Commercial Service (CCS) became Government Commercial Agency (GCA) on 1 April 2026. Documents published before this date may still refer to CCS. All contract documents remain valid for existing and new contracts, and there are no changes to how you work with us.

Core terms and conditions

The core terms form part of the framework and call-off contracts and must be used on every contract. They cannot be changed.

The core terms are GCA’s standard commercial terms. They govern the supplier’s relationship with GCA and every customer who has a call-off contract.
