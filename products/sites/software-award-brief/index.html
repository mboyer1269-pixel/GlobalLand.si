Three UK public software contract notices, explained for software suppliers

These are awarded or proposed purchases, not open tenders. None of them can be bid for through these notices. Where a buyer mentions a future procurement we quote it; we do not infer dates, routes or eligibility.

Sources: the official Find a Tender OCDS API, one release package per notice (linked below). The HTML notice pages returned HTTP 403 to our fetcher, so we did not read them; that does not mean they lack information.

1. Croydon Health Services NHS Trust: EPR extension with Oracle Health (notice 105806-2026)

  • Scope: "continued provision of the core EPR software and associated clinical applications, application management, hosting, software licensing, maintenance and technical support services", "commencing on 29th December 2026 for an initial period of three years, with an option to extend for a further 12 months".
  • Value: "The estimated total value of the proposed extension, including the optional fourth year, is approximately £17,549,439 excluding VAT."
  • Route: "Award procedure without prior publication of a call for competition", on grounds of "technical compatibility, interoperability and the significant technical and operational difficulties associated with introducing an alternative supplier". "No competitive evaluation has been undertaken in relation to the proposed extension."
  • Status: an advance transparency notice. The buyer says the 5 November 2026 date in the notice "does not represent the date of contract conclusion" and "The proposed extension has not yet been executed". "The current contract expires on 28th December 2026."
  • Future procurement (quoted): "The Trust is separately progressing its longer-term EPR strategy, which will include a competitive procurement for its longer-term EPR requirements." This release publishes no date, procedure or eligibility for it, and we infer none.
  • Source: OCDS release package 105806-2026

2. London Borough of Haringey: housing mobile workforce software from Totalmobile (notice 105970-2026)

  • Scope: "For the provision of a mobile workforce management system for Housing Repairs and Maintenance." Main CPV 48100000 (industry specific software package); additional CPV 72261000 (software support services).
  • Value as published: GBP 406,605.60 (the notice does not say whether VAT or options are included). Contract signed 2026-10-03. Bids received: 1.
  • Route: awarded "as a direct award under a framework agreement" under "Regulation 33(8)(a) of the Public Contracts Regulations 2015". The framework is not named in the release. Options: "Contract Extension" (no detail).
  • Our interpretation: this purchase went through an existing framework rather than a public call for competition.
  • Source: OCDS release package 105970-2026

3. Scottish Police Authority: EV charging back office from Hanger19 Limited (notice 105743-2026)

  • Scope: a "Single Back-Office Solution for the Police Scotland EV Charging Infrastructure", including charge point management software.
  • Value as published: GBP 600,000 (VAT and lifetime not stated). Contract signed 2026-10-23. Open procedure; award criteria quality 60, price 40; 9 bids, all reported as SME bids; the winner is reported as an SME. No options.
  • Detailed requirements are in tender documents on Public Contracts Scotland, which we did not read.
  • Source: OCDS release package 105743-2026

Data quirk: in notices 105970-2026 and 105743-2026 the structured lot status reads "cancelled" while the award and contract read "active". We do not interpret this; check the official notice before relying on either.
