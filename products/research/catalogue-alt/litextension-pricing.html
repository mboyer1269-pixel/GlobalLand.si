Frequently Asked Questions

Not seeing your question listed here? Contact us for the quickest answer.
How much does the Automated Migration Tool cost?

The LitExtension Automated Migration Tool starts from $19 per project, and the final price is determined by the total number of entities you select to migrate.

With this plan, you get up to 6 Additional Options for free, while advanced options such as Customer Password Migration (applicable for selected platforms), SEO URL Migration, or 301 Redirect are paid add-ons. 

How much does the All-in-One Migration Service cost?

LitExtension All-in-One Migration Service is priced based on your entity count, plus the complexity of your migration, starting from $129 per project. 

With our AIO package, you’ll get a full migration customized to match your requirements, free ALL Additional Options, and expert support from our Personal Assistant. For an exact quote for your migration project, please contact our Customer Support Agents via live chat or email.

Why does the All-in-One have a higher price than the Automated ?

The All-in-One Migration service is a whole package when it:

  • Handles most complicated migration cases that Automated Migration can’t.
  • Assigns a Personal Assistant who will handle everything in the migration process for you so you can spend time and effort on other important tasks involved in your new store operations.
  • Offers a much quicker response time compared to the Automated Migration, whether on weekdays or weekends.
Is there any additional cost after purchasing LitExtension service?

If you buy the Automated Migration, you will have to pay extra fees for certain additional options such as Customer Password Migration, SEO URLs Migration, and 301 Redirect, as well as special data customizations. Also, the after-migration like Re-migration, Recent Migration, and Smart Update may require extra fees. 

Instead, if you purchase our All-in-One Migration Package (AIO), many additional options are included. Our Personal Assistant (PA), who comes with the AIO Migration Package, will also provide technical support via email for free within 30 days after the Full Migration.

What security is in place to protect my information?

We have a set of policies, including a Data Security Policy, GDPR Compliance, and Privacy Policy, to guarantee data security in every project we handle. 

We take full responsiblity for and pride ourselves on protecting our customer data in all forms, on all media, and during all phases of the migration process.

Can I receive support if I buy the Automated Migration Package?

You will receive support when you buy any kind of service from LitExtension. We commit to response within 24 hours after receiving your request. 

    I forgot to use a coupon & paid full price. Can I get my money back?

    Unfortunately, if you forget to apply a discount code during the checkout at the Order Confirmation step, the discount will not be applied and LitExtension will not refund this discount amount.

    However, the good news is that we will add this amount back to your balance manually in your next migrations, please chat with us during your migration and before your discount code expires.

    What are the conditions for the Free Demo Migration?

    LItExtension Free Demo Migration is free to run and unlimited in number of attempts, but each demo transfers only a sample of your data: up to 20 products, 20 customers, and 20 orders, plus their related data, from your Source Store to the Target Store. This is enough to preview how your store will look and confirm the setup works correctly before you commit to the Full Migration.

    Running a new demo doesn’t affect your original data. It simply refreshes that same sample set, and once you proceed with the Full Migration, all your selected entities transfer completely.

    Can I migrate with LitExtension for free?

    Yes, you can use our Automated Migration Tool for free if your store has fewer than 100 products, 100 customers, and 100 orders. On top of that, you still get unlimited demo migrations to preview the results, and then one complete Full Migration of all your products, customers, and orders at no cost, with no downtime to your store during the process.

    If your store exceeds this limit, no worries. You can still migrate with LitExtension’s Automated Migration Tool; the price starts at $19.

    Is LitExtension’s service one-time purchase or monthly fee?

    All of our Migration services are One-Time purchases. However, we do offer the Premium Support Service that require monthly payment. 

    How can I get the Invoice after purchasing?

    I am getting error during payment in Paypal

    If you don’t see the Pay with Debit or Credit Card option on the Paypal login page, you may see the Create an Account option while logging into Paypal. Click on it to start the registration. Paypay announced the main reasons for this event as it runs these risk checks to determine the eligibility for Guest Checkout:

    • Buyer’s country/location
    • Buyer’s purchase history
    • Other internal risks

    Also, Guest Checkout is unavailable for some countries to avoid chargeback/fraud issues like India, Brazil, etc.

    If you get an error called Got Http response code 400 when accessing while paying by Paypal, please check again and make sure that your Paypal account works normally and the balance is greater than the amount you need to pay. This error is caused by 2 main reasons:

    • Your Paypal account is not enough to make a payment
    • Your Paypal account is declined for payment

    If you still have problems with Paypal, please look for other payment methods in our Payment Guide.

    My coupon does not work. Why?

    Valid coupon codes are displayed as announcements on the LitExtension website and LitExtension social network channels (Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter). You can use coupons provided by our official partners as well. Any other promo codes you find on the internet are fake since we don’t offer coupons through deal sites.

    If your coupon code does not work, there are some main reasons:

    • Coupon has expired
    • It is not from our official partners.
    • It is from deal sites
