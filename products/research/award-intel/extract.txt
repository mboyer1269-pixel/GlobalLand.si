Head to our full FAQs page , or get in touch at contact@tussell.com What is Tussell? Tussell is the leading public sector market intelligence platform. It's trusted by suppliers to help them win more business with government. The platform consolidates and enriches all UK public procurement data into one searchable, easy-to-use platform. With this single source of truth, Tussell empowers suppliers with the market insights they need to grow their public sector business. How can Tussell help me grow in the UK public sector? Tussell gives you a complete, real-time view of the UK public sector market, empowering you to: Discover new & upcoming tenders in your niche before your competitors Accurately size your market and understand where the biggest opportunities lie Analyse your target accounts’ spending patterns and existing supplier relationships Track competitors’ contract wins, renewals and framework positions Identify which framework agreements your target buyers actually use Build relationships early by finding key decision-makers and engaging before opportunities go live What data does Tussell cover? Tussell brings together live and historic data from every stage of the UK public procurement process - covering early buying signals, tenders, contract awards, framework agreements, spending, and key buyer contact details. But Tussell isn't just a data aggregator - we enrich and verify the data to make it decision-ready. We remove duplicates, manually link call-off contracts to their parent frameworks, and carry out extensive research to fill in missing details. This commitment to accuracy and completeness is why Tussell is trusted by government itself to track their own procurement - and why leading suppliers rely on our data to make strategic decisions. Who uses Tussell? Tussell is used by suppliers of all industries and sizes - from the UK Government's 'Strategic Suppliers' to up-and-coming SMEs - to understand and win more business in the public sector. Within these organisations, teams focused on public sector sales, business development, account management, capture planning, research, and bids & tender management get the most value from Tussell's insights. Tussell is also trusted by the public sector itself - including the Cabinet Office and Crown Commercial Service - as well as by national media outlets when analysing and reporting on public procurement. How does Tussell differ to Find a Tender & other paid platforms? Open portals - like Find a Tender - let suppliers view newly published tenders and early market engagements. Some paid tools simply aggregate these open sources. Tussell goes much further. It covers the full UK public procurement cycle - from early buying signals and tenders to contract awards, frameworks and invoices - giving suppliers a complete view of their market to build a proactive, data-driven sales strategy. Tussell is not an e-procurement, bid management or AI bid-writing tool - it is a market intelligence platform designed to give suppliers the insight they need to win more public sector business. How much does Tussell cost? Tussell is a top-of-the-line market intelligence platform. Because of the quality, depth and reliability of our data, an annual subscription to Tussell typically costs more than basic tender monitoring tools. Pricing varies depending on which Tussell Tier you choose and any additional modules or integrations you add - such as our Early Opportunities module, Unlimited Key Decision Maker contact credits, or API access. Every subscription comes with a named Account Manager, who's there to ensure your team get maximum value from the platform. How can I get access to Tussell? Tussell is accessible via an annual subscription. To learn more about how you and your team could benefit from Tussell access, book in time with our Sales team.
