What are the procurement stages?
Open opportunities
- Planning or Tender stage notices with deadlines in the future
Pipeline
- Potential future contracts and dynamic markets - required for contracts over £2 million

- Early information or engagement about future contracts - bids not yet possible
Tender
- Bids can be submitted until the deadline
Award
- Contract award decisions have been made

- Contracts have been signed or will be changed
Termination
- Procurement processes, contracts or dynamic markets have ended (including successful completion)

Definitions
Framework
- A contract that allows the award of future contracts, which will be advertised only to suppliers on the framework.
Dynamic market
- Arrangements that suppliers who meet the conditions for membership can join, which allow them to bid for future contracts restricted to members of the market. Future contracts must be advertised in tender notices. New suppliers can join at any time during the term of the market.
Utilities dynamic market
- A dynamic market that can only be used by utility organisations to award utilities contracts. Tender notices may optionally be provided only to members of the market. (Not included in the DM/DPS filter option.)
Dynamic purchasing system
- Similar to a dynamic market, but only allowed for commonly used purchases. Tender notices are not required for future contracts. New suppliers can join at any time during the term of the market. Established under the Public Contracts Regulations 2015 or Utilities Contracts Regulations 2016.
Qualification system
- Similar to a utilities dynamic market, a system used by utilities to pre-qualify suppliers. Established under the Utilities Contracts Regulations 2016.
